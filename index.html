About Mara

Warm, personal photography you'll actually enjoy

Habana Studios is a home-based, mobile photography business built on one idea: beautiful images without a complicated or intimidating process. I specialize in on-location sessions across Gainesville, UF campus, Santa Fe College, and surrounding areas.

Whether you're crossing the stage at graduation, celebrating a milestone with family, or planning an intimate elopement — I'll guide you through every pose, every location, every detail. You don't need to know how to be in front of a camera. That's my job.
