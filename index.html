Behind the lens

I'm Mara — and I photograph
the chapters worth keeping.

For five years I've been chasing golden hour around Gainesville, learning faces, learning light, learning how to make people feel themselves in front of a camera. I'm currently finishing my degree in photography, which means I bring the discipline of a student and the heart of a friend to every shoot.

My specialty is graduates — UF alumni in front of Century Tower, Santa Fe students on campus paths, families gathering for the moment everyone worked toward together. But honestly? I'm here to make anyone in this town happy with the photos they came to me for.

"The goal isn't a perfect photo — it's a real one you'll still love in twenty years."
